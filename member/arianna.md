Hello! My name is Arianna Possié, and I’m proud to be part of the team at Aracely’s Daycare—as well as Aracely’s granddaughter! I’ve always had a strong passion for working with young children and creating a positive, nurturing environment for their growth.

Before joining the daycare, I worked as a school aide in the Weber School District, supporting at-risk children. I also had the meaningful opportunity to serve an 18-month mission in Guatemala for the LDS Church, where I developed deeper empathy, patience, and cultural understanding.

I’ve completed early childhood education courses at Weber High School and continue to build on that foundation with hands-on experience and a heart for helping each child feel safe, loved, and supported.
